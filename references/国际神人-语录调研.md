# 国际神人语录调研

## 哈维尔·米莱 Javier Milei

**人物背景：** 阿根廷总统（2023年至今），无政府资本主义者，以电锯为竞选道具著称

**达沃斯2024演讲（WEF官方记录）：**
- "¡Viva la libertad, carajo!" （自由万岁，他妈的！）
- "The state does not create wealth; the state destroys it. The state can give you nothing because it produces nothing."
- "Free trade capitalism is the only instrument we have to end hunger, poverty and extreme poverty across our planet. The empirical evidence is unquestionable."
- "The main leaders of the Western world have abandoned the model of freedom for different versions of what we call collectivism."
- "Collectivist experiments are never the solution to the problems that afflict the citizens of the world — rather they are the root cause."

**联合国大会演讲：**
- "Socialism is always and everywhere an impoverishing phenomenon that murdered over 100 million human beings."
- "There are no market failures. The only context for market failure is coercion, and only the state holds a monopoly on violence."
- "My contempt for the state is infinite."

**其他语录（Wikiquote）：**
- "If printing money would end poverty, printing diplomas would end stupidity."
- "Countries that have more freedom are 12 times richer than those that are repressed."
- "A successful entrepreneur is a hero."
- "Not only have we stopped feeling like losers, we also know that we can make Argentina great again."
- "You don't get out of poverty by magic. You get out of poverty with capitalism, savings and hard work."
- "The only way out of poverty is more freedom."

**语言特征分析：**
米莱的演讲风格极度高强度——语速快、情绪高亢、重复核心词汇（libertad、casta、mercado）。
达沃斯演讲被视为近年最极端的右翼自由市场宣言。
他对中央银行的厌恶近乎宗教性质，任何货币政策讨论都会触发这一话题。

## 泽连斯基 Volodymyr Zelensky

**人物背景：** 乌克兰总统，前喜剧演员，2022年俄乌战争爆发后成为全球最知名领导人之一

**最著名语录（CNN/WashPost核实）：**
- "The fight is here; I need ammunition, not a ride."（2022年2月，美方提出撤离时的回应）
- "We are not putting down arms. We will be defending our country, because our weapon is truth, and our truth is that this is our land, our country, our children."
- "I really do not want my pictures in your offices. Hang your kids' photos instead, and look at them each time you are making a decision."

**议会演讲系列：**
- "We Ukrainians are a peaceful nation. But if we remain silent today, we will be gone tomorrow!"
- "We will win. Glory to Ukraine."（标准结尾语）
- "We must become Icelanders in soccer, Israelis in defending our land, Japanese in technology."
- "Who is ready to fight alongside us? I don't see anyone."

**其他语录：**
- "I am not a politician. I am just a simple person who has come to break down this system."
- "Ukraine is alive. Ukraine lives. Ukraine fights. Ukraine advances."
- "It's a victory when the weapons fall silent and people speak up."
- "People believe in words only for a stretch of time. Then they start to look for action."
- "We want to determine our history by ourselves. In peace, calm and honesty."

**语言特征分析：**
泽连斯基的演讲经过专业撰稿人润色，但保留了他作为演员的表演感。
"我在这里"（I am here）是贯穿整个战争时期的核心符号。
他擅长用对比修辞："我们需要弹药，不需要撤退"——简短、有力、上口。

## 唐纳德·川普 Donald Trump

**人物背景：** 美国第45任、第47任总统，商人，《学徒》节目主持人

**标志性金句：**
- "You're fired!"（《学徒》节目口头禅，后成为政治符号）
- "Make America Great Again."（竞选口号）
- "Drain the Swamp."（清除政治腐败口号）
- "I alone can fix it."（2016年共和党提名演讲）
- "Never Surrender."（2024年口号）

**思维与商业语录（BrainyQuote）：**
- "You have to think anyway, so why not think big?"
- "My whole life is about winning. I don't lose often. I almost never lose."
- "My motto is: Always get even. When somebody screws you, screw them back in spades."
- "I like thinking big. If you're going to be thinking anything, you might as well think big."

**政治语录：**
- "We will build a great wall along the southern border — and Mexico will pay for the wall."
- "We're going to win so much, you're going to be so sick and tired of winning."
- "I will be the greatest jobs president that God ever created."
- "In America, we don't worship government. We worship God."

**语言特征分析：**
川普的语言风格是故意的口语化和重复——"tremendous"、"beautiful"、"disaster"、"witch hunt"反复出现。
第三人称自我指代（"Trump did this"）在政治人物中极为罕见，强化了他的品牌属性。
每个句子都倾向于用最高级，没有中间地带。

## 埃隆·马斯克 Elon Musk

**人物背景：** 特斯拉CEO、SpaceX创始人、X（前推特）老板

**第一性原理思维（多处采访）：**
- "I tend to approach things from a physics framework. Physics teaches you to reason from first principles rather than by analogy."
- "You boil things down to the most fundamental truths and then reason up from there."
- "It is important to view knowledge as a semantic tree — make sure you understand the fundamental principles before you get into the leaves."

**工作与坚持：**
- "When something is important enough, you do it even if the odds are not in your favor."
- "Work like hell. You just have to put in 80 to 100 hour weeks every week."
- "Persistence is very important. You should not give up unless forced to."
- "I wasn't willing to sacrifice my game, but I also wasn't willing to sacrifice my family time. So I sacrificed sleep."（科比引用此话）

**火星与愿景：**
- "I would like to die on Mars. Just not on impact."
- "Making life multi-planetary is the most important thing we can do."

**人生哲学：**
- "I'd rather be optimistic and wrong than pessimistic and right."
- "If the rules are such that you can't make progress, fight the rules."
- "Life is too short for long-term grudges."
- "If you get up in the morning and think the future is going to be better, it is a bright day."

**语言特征分析：**
马斯克的推特风格和演讲风格是两套完全不同的系统。
演讲时用工程师语言，严谨、量化；推特上用表情包、单词回复、随机@人。
"First principles"是他最常用的思维框架，几乎所有问题都会被引导到这里。

## 科比·布莱恩特 Kobe Bryant

**人物背景：** NBA洛杉矶湖人队传奇后卫，五届NBA总冠军，Mamba Mentality创始人，2020年意外去世

**Mamba Mentality核心语录：**
- "To sum up what Mamba Mentality is: it means to be able to constantly try to be the best version of yourself."
- "I can't relate to lazy people. We don't speak the same language. I don't understand you. I don't want to understand you."
- "I have nothing in common with lazy people who blame others for their lack of success. Great things come from hard work and perseverance. No excuses."

**关于失败与成长：**
- "Everything negative — pressure, challenges — is all an opportunity for me to rise."
- "There's nothing truly to be afraid of, when you think about it, because I've failed before, and I woke up the next morning, and I'm OK."
- "If you're afraid to fail, then you're probably going to fail."

**关于领导力：**
- "Leadership is lonely. I'm not going to be afraid of confrontation to get us to where we need to go."
- "The moment you give up is the moment you let someone else win."
- "A lot of people say they want to be great, but they're not willing to make the sacrifices necessary to achieve greatness."

**关于睡眠与训练：**
- "I wasn't willing to sacrifice my game, but I also wasn't willing to sacrifice my family time. So I sacrificed sleep."
- "If you want to be a better player, you have to prepare, prepare, and prepare some more."

**"凌晨四点"典故：**
科比曾多次描述自己比任何人都起得早训练。
"洛杉矶凌晨四点"成为网络上关于极致自律的标志性意象。

## 珍妮特·耶伦 Janet Yellen

**人物背景：** 美联储前主席（2014-2018），美国财政部长（2021-2025）

**"暂时性通胀"系列：**
- "We believe inflation is transitory."（2021年，后被现实打脸）
- "I was wrong about the path inflation would take."（2022年，罕见公开认错）

**标准美联储语言：**
- "The labor market remains robust and resilient."
- "We expect a soft landing for the economy."
- "This requires careful and prudent monitoring over an extended period."
- "There are upside risks to inflation that bear watching."

**语言特征分析：**
耶伦代表了美联储官员的独特话语体系：高度限定、双向对冲、永远留有余地。
"transitory"成为她最著名的失误预测词，被经济界反复引用。
她的每句话都会被市场解读，因此语言极度谨慎，但偶尔的"失误"代价极大。
